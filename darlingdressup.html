Darling Dress Up is a game demo inspired by nostalgic browser games I've played in my childhood during quarantine, from CoolMathGames to classic online dress up websites where I spent hours dressing Barbie dolls and princesses. This project combines that sense of nostalgia with the monochromatic gothic art style I’ve since developed, connecting my past childhood memories with my current artistic style.
