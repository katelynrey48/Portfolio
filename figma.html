Building the foundations of this website

I drew inspiration from the early 2000s aesthetic, particularly the look of My Chemical Romance, and originally designed the header so each page would scroll like a slideshow. As I tested it, I realized this style was unintuitive for visitors, so I switched to a more familiar layout that's easier to navigate. I kept the gothic aesthetic throughout, so the site still reflects my overall design vision and personal taste.

Tiktok ad prototype

During my data analysis internship at Warby Parker, I led a group project that used Excel to analyze a period of rising sales and Figma to explain what was driving it. I designed a mock TikTok ad as an interactive prototype that follows a customer's journey, from the first moments of scrolling, to exploring the product, to checking out. Pairing that visual walkthrough with the spreadsheet analysis turned raw numbers into a story anyone could follow.

I presented the project to Warby Parker's Chief of Technology and his team, walking them through both the data and the ad experience. The project combined my design background with my growing data skills, and it showed me how a well-designed ad can help explain the "why" behind a trend.

Designing an online flower shop

Flowers is a mobile shopping concept I designed in Figma for a fictional flower shop. The product page for Purple Roses features a rose illustration that doubles as the "F" in the logo, a "2-for-1 Sale" banner, a search bar, and a simple quantity selector with an add-to-cart button. The palette pairs a deep burgundy ( #5E0518) with rich purple ( #9A5F9D) and soft lavender ( #CDC2D8), so the design feels romantic without being harsh. For type, I used Island Moments for the title and Instrument Serif for everything else. I refined the layout through a second iteration focused on usability. My first version had cramped text with almost no line spacing or padding, so I added better margins and breathing room to make the product description easier to read on a small screen.
